// 04 · how i think

I think in systems,
not just endpoints.

Every project starts on a whiteboard: where does the request go, what can fail, what's cached, what the database actually has to do. This is the shape most of my backends take.

01

Cache the hot path

Redis in front of repeated reads, TTLs tuned per entity, eviction on write — never stale, never slow.

02

Authorize everything

JWT at the gateway, role checks at the endpoint. No trust-me routes.

03

Migrate, don't mutate

Versioned Flyway migrations. The schema's history is as important as the schema.

04

Fail loudly, recover cleanly

Centralized exception handling, validated inputs, meaningful status codes.
